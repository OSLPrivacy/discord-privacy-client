claim supported: OSL timed delete cannot recall an email after normal delivery

Microsoft Support says personal email accounts cannot use message recall and, once sent, the message is outside the sender's control. Its "Undo send" feature delays sending rather than recalling a delivered message:
https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/mail/how-to-recall-an-email-in-outlook-requirements-limitations-steps

Microsoft documents a narrow Microsoft 365 same-organization, unopened-message exception. OSL does not operate that Exchange-only facility, so it cannot promise or perform recall for email. This proof supports the product warning's OSL/timed-delete scope; it does not claim that no proprietary mail server has any administrative recall feature.
