checked 2026-08-08
claim supported: timed delete acts on the other person's visible copy through the messaging app's own delete-for-everyone operation

Signal Support names the in-app operation "Delete for everyone" and describes it as a best-effort delete on every device in that chat:
https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360050426432-Delete-for-everyone

Telegram's official FAQ says its in-app delete can delete a one-to-one message "for both sides":
https://telegram.org/faq#q-can-i-delete-my-messages

Messenger Help distinguishes "Delete for you" from the app's "Delete for everyone" and says the latter makes the message no longer visible on other people's chat screens:
https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/194400311449172/

scope note: this supports removal from the other person's app screen, not removal of screenshots, notification history, exports, backups, reports, or any other copy outside that app operation
