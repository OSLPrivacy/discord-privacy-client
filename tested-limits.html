Tested limits

This is the long form of the problem list on what OSL can prove. Every entry names the status the problem carries, the record that established it, and the files you can open to check it.

Entries come from three places and nothing else: the capability registry in data/pricing.json, the versioned public support matrix in docs/status/support-matrix.json, and findings written down with the file that establishes them. A capability that is not marked Available, or a service the support matrix refuses to let us claim, appears here automatically.

Known problems named on this page: 39. Entries in the limits record data/tested-limits.json: 39.

Every entry

  1. Per-message hybrid sealing

    Status: Beta

    Working. Every direct message is sealed to the recipient keys with a fresh key of its own, combining X25519 with post-quantum ML-KEM-768. Proven on test builds against a real conversation, not yet on a numbered release build.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  2. Cover text carrier

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. The readable text never reaches the connected service, but what the service currently receives is an obvious encrypted block rather than an innocuous-looking message. The natural-language cover mode is switched off in the current build because the post-quantum wire is too large for it.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  3. Protected text send and read

    Status: Beta

    Working on Discord. Sending and reading protected text is proven on test builds. Discord is the only connector qualified so far.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  4. Encrypted image sending

    Status: Planned

    Not in the shipping app yet. The encryption for images is written and the picker accepts PNG and JPEG, but nothing in the released app drives it end to end. This is not part of what a Pro purchase gives you today.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  5. Non-image file sending

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Only PNG and JPEG are offered; other file types are not supported.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  6. Group chat and server channel protection

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Group and channel protection is switched off in the shipping app, and the messages it would build carry no signature identifying the sender. Treat anything you write in a group or channel as unprotected.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  7. Old-message protection

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. The mechanism that would keep old messages safe if a key is later stolen is written and tested but deliberately switched off pending independent review.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  8. Attachment Privacy Guard

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Stripping hidden details out of a file before encrypting it is written but nothing in the shipping app calls it. Assume a file you send still carries its original details.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  9. Before-send warning

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. The detection code exists and is tested, but nothing in the shipping app calls it, so OSL does not warn you today.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  10. Timed expiry

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Timed deletion is not wired into the shipping app.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  11. View once

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Components are built; the production wiring and the two-party consent it requires are incomplete.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  12. Burn

    Status: Planned

    Not yet as a peer action. When it lands, burn deletes OSL local and server-side state and asks the other side to delete too. It does not destroy anyone ability to decrypt what the service already holds, and it cannot recall a screenshot.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  13. Link protection

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. This is a design intention with no code behind it.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  14. Scrub discovery

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Current source can fall back to generic JSON when a provider parser refuses an export, so provider identity, archive inventory, media bytes and completeness are not reliably bound. Google, Discord, Meta, X and WhatsApp exports are not qualified end to end.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  15. Guided deletion handoff

    Status: Planned

    Not yet, and further off than a dependency on discovery would suggest. The part of the app that would actually carry out a guided deletion is not connected: it holds every candidate and does not open the page, choose the delete action, confirm it, or check that it worked. When it does land, OSL will take you to the page and you confirm each removal yourself — OSL will not delete on your behalf.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  16. AutoScrub

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. What exists is switched-off interface scaffolding, not a working engine — the app itself says it is unavailable in this build. AutoScrub is also the one optional closed-source module, it is not installed by default, and it would need your explicit consent to download.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  17. AI-generated carrier text

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. Opt-in AI-written cover text. Cloud generation is never end-to-end private, because the service has to see the text it is working on.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  18. Processing credits

    Status: Planned

    Not yet, and not on sale. Credits would be bought separately and never renew.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  19. Automatic Pro expiry

    Status: Planned

    Not yet. A purchased code currently unlocks Pro without a redemption clock, so the month does not yet start when you enter the code.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  20. Country exposure comparison

    Status: Illustration

    A drawing, not a measurement. The homepage comparison summarises published agency and company practice. It is not a scan of your device, and this site never shows a live protection score.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  21. Website Scrub

    Status: Illustration

    Illustration only. The website does not scan browser history, read browser data, or access local account data.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

  22. Product animations

    Status: Illustration

    Drawings, not recordings. Every animated scene on this site illustrates intended behaviour rather than showing the app running.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/capability_registry. Evidence: data/pricing.json.

    Recorded by docs/status/support-matrix.json#/versioned_public_support_matrix/entries. Evidence: docs/status/support-matrix.json, docs/design/osl-master-decision-2026-07-26.md.

  24. WhatsApp — WhatsApp for Windows adapter

    Status: coming_soon

    WhatsApp needs its own runtime proof; OSL must refuse support claims until a complete adapter contract and two-peer exact-build proof exist.

    Recorded by docs/status/support-matrix.json#/versioned_public_support_matrix/entries. Evidence: docs/status/support-matrix.json, docs/design/osl-master-decision-2026-07-26.md.

  25. Telegram — Telegram Desktop native Windows adapter

    Status: externally_blocked

    This status records OSL's release gate and never a technical block in Telegram's client. Telegram Desktop cannot be listed as supported until a signed-client UI Automation probe proves stable, text-exposed message rows on an actual conversation surface AND OSL's own adapter proves live delivery: the probe half was measured on 2026-08-04 and is met; the delivery half is not, and it is the half that keeps the label.

    Recorded by docs/status/support-matrix.json#/versioned_public_support_matrix/entries. Evidence: docs/status/support-matrix.json, docs/reports/telegram-adapter-verdict.md.

  26. OSL Mail — OSL Mail for Outlook-connected mailboxes

    Status: unsupported

    Outlook is scoped as OSL Mail, not Outlook chat support; protected inline replies must refuse until a separate OSL Mail gate proves mailbox binding, recipient authority, safe send behavior, draft handling, and runtime support.

    Recorded by docs/status/support-matrix.json#/versioned_public_support_matrix/entries. Evidence: docs/status/support-matrix.json, docs/reports/outlook-osl-mail-verdict.md.

  27. Key substitution after a reinstall

    Status: Open critical finding

    The safety number you compare with a friend covers only the signing key, so a key server that swapped a recipient's encryption keys would not change the number you check. Until the whole key bundle is covered, the verification ceremony does not protect the keys that decrypt messages.

    Recorded by docs/THREAT_MODEL.md. Evidence: docs/THREAT_MODEL.md.

  28. No outside cryptographic review has been paid for

    Status: Open

    The encryption that ships is a custom construction, and no outside firm has been commissioned to examine it. A paid third-party cryptographic review is a prerequisite for v1 stable, not something that has happened.

    Recorded by docs/THREAT_MODEL.md#audit-status-v1-alpha-vs-v1-stable. Evidence: docs/THREAT_MODEL.md, docs/security/osl-audit-2026-07-26-codex.md.

  29. OSL-RN is unreviewed research and carries no real traffic

    Status: Planned

    The next-generation ratchet crate is reachable only from its own tests, has had no outside cryptographic review, and the shipping runtime keeps its gate closed, so no real message goes through it.

    Recorded by crates/osl-ratchet-next/DESIGN.md. Evidence: crates/osl-ratchet-next/DESIGN.md, Cargo.toml.

  30. The Windows build is not code signed

    Status: Open

    The Windows executable is not signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation warning. The minisign updater key and the published checksum are the only build-integrity anchors today.

    Recorded by docs/release/code-signing-decision.md. Evidence: docs/release/code-signing-decision.md, docs/download.html.

  31. Rebuilding a release compares the program, not the installer

    Status: Open

    The rebuild guide compares the application executable inside the installer and deliberately does not compare the installer bytes or the updater signature, so a match proves the program came from the published source and nothing more.

    Recorded by docs/release/verify-the-build-yourself.md. Evidence: docs/release/verify-the-build-yourself.md.

  32. Device-removal detection is Windows-only and unmeasured

    Status: Open

    The removal trigger is Windows-only, it cannot see a removal while the machine is suspended or hibernating, and its delivery delay has never been measured on a virtual machine, so no deadline is promised.

    Recorded by docs/design/device-removal-detection-limits.md. Evidence: docs/design/device-removal-detection-limits.md.

  33. Recorded delete-for-everyone windows are research, not proof

    Status: Research only

    The saved per-service delete-for-everyone windows are looked-up limits from each provider, and the record says outright that it does not prove timed delete works in OSL.

    Recorded by proof/research/timed-delete-limits.txt. Evidence: proof/research/timed-delete-limits.txt.

  34. Hosted Android workspace is unavailable

    Status: Planned

    A hosted Android workspace is not offered here. It would need its own threat model, its own explicit consent, and a fresh review before any claim about it changed.

    Recorded by apps/osl-hub-ui/src/android-workspace.test.ts. Evidence: apps/osl-hub-ui/src/android-workspace.test.ts, apps/osl-hub-ui/src/main.ts.

  35. AutoScrub drives the service interface and can cost you the account

    Status: Planned

    AutoScrub works by driving the service's own interface. That may break the service's rules and could terminate your account, so it is never installed without your explicit consent to that risk.

    Recorded by apps/osl-hub-ui/src/component-consent.ts. Evidence: apps/osl-hub-ui/src/component-consent.ts, apps/osl-hub-ui/src/component-consent.test.ts.

  36. Buying Pro is not consent to cloud processing

    Status: Open

    Pro on its own never turns on cloud generation. A cloud request is refused with cloud-consent-required until you give a separate, explicit consent, and basic encryption and the word-bank carrier never depend on Pro.

    Recorded by apps/osl-hub-ui/src/cover-generation-eligibility.test.ts. Evidence: apps/osl-hub-ui/src/cover-generation-eligibility.test.ts, docs/design/pro-activation-onboarding.md.

  37. A Pro code has no redemption clock

    Status: Open

    No redemption record exists yet, so the keyserver cannot start a month when you enter a code and cannot return an account to Free by itself.

    Recorded by data/pricing.json#/required_phrases. Evidence: data/pricing.json, docs/design/osl-master-decision-2026-07-26.md.

  38. The connected service still sees who and when

    Status: Open

    A connected service receives an encrypted block instead of your words, but it still sees who you talk to, when, and how often, and OSL cannot hide that.

    Recorded by docs/design/osl-public-claim-allowlist.md#a2. Evidence: docs/design/osl-public-claim-allowlist.md, docs/THREAT_MODEL.md.

  39. Burn cannot reach the copy the service holds

    Status: Planned

    Burn erases OSL's own local copy and may ask the other side to delete theirs. The encrypted block the service already stored stays readable to anyone holding the recipient's long-term keys, and no burn recalls a screenshot.

    Recorded by docs/design/osl-public-claim-allowlist.md#d. Evidence: docs/design/osl-public-claim-allowlist.md.
