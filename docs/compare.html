OSL and private messengers

OSL is a hub over existing accounts, not a purpose-built messaging network. These products are not equivalent.

Signal may be the better choice when you need a purpose-built private messenger.

This page is not a scan of your device. This site never shows a live protection score.

Signal

Signal describes itself as a private messenger. Its official documentation says usernames can be used without sharing a phone number.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp says personal messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. Its privacy policy describes the information it collects and uses.

Telegram

Telegram distinguishes device-specific Secret Chats from Cloud Chats; their security properties are different.

OSL

OSL capability claims are limited by current status evidence. It is not a replacement for a purpose-built private messenger.
