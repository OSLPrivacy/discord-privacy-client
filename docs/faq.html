Frequently asked questions

Should I use OSL instead of Signal?

Signal may be the better choice if you need a purpose-built private messenger. OSL is a hub over existing accounts and is not equivalent to Signal.

See the sourced messenger comparison for the limits of that comparison.

Can OSL get my account banned?

Yes, that is a real risk on some connected services. OSL has no provider approval, and using OSL with Discord may violate Discord's Terms of Service. Discord may ban your account. OSL does not publish a ban-risk percentage or promise that assisted deletion is safe from provider sanctions.

Does OSL make bad messages safe to send?

No. OSL is not a moderation system, legal advice, or permission to send abusive, threatening, illegal, or otherwise harmful messages. Connected services can still enforce their rules, and recipients can still report, copy, screenshot, export, or retain what they see.

What happens when I stop AutoScrub?

AutoScrub is not available in this build. The intended design is bounded: it applies only to a previously approved scope and stops on limits, challenges, changed content, or failed checks. If a stop is already in progress, OSL is stopping after the checked local items already in review.

Does Burn log me out or delete connected-service history?

No. Burn covers OSL-controlled local state, not the external browser or native profile. The service registry row, external browser/native profile, login session, cookies, and native carrier history are never members of the Burn manifest. Unlink/logout is a separate user action.

What does Pro include today?

Pro is an early-access purchase. Today it unlocks early access to new privacy tools as they reach the beta channel, not the v1 feature set, which is still being finished. Today that means protected text on Discord with no message limit, plus new privacy tools first as they reach the public beta channel. Encrypted images, other file types, AutoScrub, view once, expiry, burn, and AI-written cover text arrive at v1.

What are the pricing facts?

$5 is the intended price for one month of Pro. Nothing renews automatically and there is nothing to cancel. OSL never stores payment details, including for repeat purchases, and no OSL account is required. Card, Bitcoin and Monero all work the same way: one payment, one month. Checkout is paused until one-month redemption and expiry are implemented.
