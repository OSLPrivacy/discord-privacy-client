# OSL Requirements Addendum — 2026-07-26

Late lifecycle requirements preserve the user’s control over onboarding,
recovery, account removal, consent, and cleanup. Automation must stop safely on
changed state, limits, challenges, or failed verification. D85 remains the
authoritative exception: AutoScrub UNATTENDED is correct and must not be
relabeled attended.
