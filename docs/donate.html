Donate to OSL

Donations support OSL. They help fund public builds, audits, and operating costs.

Donations do not unlock Pro. Pro access is issued only through the separate prepaid Pro checkout and explicit code redemption path.

Ways to donate

Pick whichever is easiest. Each one is a single payment of $1 to $10,000, with no subscription and no OSL account.

  • Card. Pay on the hosted Stripe checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe, not to OSL.
  • Bitcoin. Pay a one-time invoice address. The amount is quoted up front and the invoice expires after 30 minutes.
  • Monero. Pay a one-time invoice address, quoted and timed the same way as Bitcoin.

A coin is offered only while this deployment accepts it, so an option you cannot see here is one you cannot pay with today.

What a donation is not

The public donation controls are separate from license activation. A donation receipt is not an activation code.

No amount and no payment method changes what the app does for you. To buy Pro, use the prepaid Pro checkout instead.
